The build source and commands are pinned. Fresh filesystem images need not have
the checked-in image's SHA-256 because historical mkfs.ubifs includes variable
metadata. Inspect and compare decoded geometry and format for rebuilt images.

The checked-in hashes are recorded in SHA256SUMS. The image is an offline
candidate for the proposed sbdata profile; it is not approved for device use.
The helper artifact is ARM EABI5, soft-float, statically linked, and stripped.
It contains no UBI resize ioctl and its install command is hard-disabled.
